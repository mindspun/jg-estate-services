Straightforward pricing

Volume-based pricing for every job

Accepted items are priced primarily by the trailer space they use. The same four volume tiers apply to residential and commercial jobs.

Request an estimate ↗Call 262-699-0100

One pricing system

Pricing is based on load volume

Accepted items are priced by how much space they take up in the trailer. The same volume-based pricing applies to homes, rental properties, offices, retail spaces, and other commercial locations.

Quarter trailer

$275

About 25% of the trailer, or roughly 3–4 cubic yards.

Half trailer

$425

About 50% of the trailer, or roughly 7–8 cubic yards.

Three-quarter trailer

$575

About 75% of the trailer, or roughly 10–11 cubic yards.

Full trailer

$750

Up to the trailer’s full capacity, or roughly 14–15 cubic yards.

Included in volume pricing

No separate TV or Freon-appliance charge

Televisions, refrigerators, freezers, and other refrigerant appliances do not currently carry a separate item surcharge. When accepted, they count toward the total trailer volume like other items.

Send photos of the full load and the property city. We’ll estimate the volume and confirm the quote before loading begins.

Items we do not take

Excluded materials and items

  • Hazardous materials or hazardous waste
  • Biohazardous, medical, or contaminated material
  • Paint, solvents, chemicals, oil, fuel, or similar liquids
  • Fluorescent bulbs or tubes
  • Asbestos or suspected asbestos-containing material
  • Vehicles

If you are unsure about an item, include it in your photos and ask before scheduling.
